\section*{Overview}
\vspace{-0.5em}
The \textbf{BM\_FGV\_Propag2.R} Shiny application is a 15-tab analytical tool supporting Brazil's PROPAG and ``Juros por Educação'' initiative, connecting labor market demand with technical education supply for evidence-based state investment plans.

\section*{Key Data Flows}
\vspace{-0.5em}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Population:} IBGE projections $\rightarrow$ \texttt{br\_pop1a.R} $\rightarrow$ age cohorts with demographic crossover
\item \textbf{Census:} School Census microdata $\rightarrow$ \texttt{matriculas1UF.R} $\rightarrow$ PNE Meta 11 tracking
\item \textbf{Finance:} FGV/Vidal Excel $\rightarrow$ \texttt{avila\_vidal01.R} $\rightarrow$ 9 PROPAG scenarios
\item \textbf{Labor:} RAIS/CAGED $\rightarrow$ multiple scripts $\rightarrow$ APLs, dynamism, shortage indicators
\item \textbf{AI Match:} CNCT+CBO $\rightarrow$ \texttt{qbq\_cnct6a.R} (GPT-4) $\rightarrow$ 218 courses $\leftrightarrow$ occupations
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Critical Dependencies}
\vspace{-0.5em}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Geographic backbone:} \texttt{df\_codes\_ibge.rda} -- 33 variables linking all datasets
\item \textbf{Enrollment core:} \texttt{meta11a\_opcoes.rda}, \texttt{df\_censo\_UF.rda} feed multiple tabs
\item \textbf{Labor market:} RAIS 2023-24 at CBO-6 digit; \textbf{AI matching:} GPT-4 embeddings (0.3 threshold)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Data Quality Notes}
\vspace{-0.5em}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item AWS S3 integration with secure credential management via \texttt{dotenv}
\item Economic dynamism: recent period (2012-2021) weighted 1.5x vs early period 1.0x
\item Shortage indicators combine salary differential, turnover, and employment stock
\item Meta 11: 3 options (PROPAG, Integrated+Concomitant, Integrated only)
\end{itemize}
